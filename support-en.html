General questions

Do I need an account?

No. None of my apps requires signing in.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. I have no access to it and therefore can’t restore it. Use the backup or export feature of the respective app, if available.

How do I delete all data of an app?

Delete the app. This removes its data from your device. Remove saved credentials (for example a token) in the app first.

A permission doesn’t work.

Check that it is turned on:

  • General (Camera, Photos, Calendar, Reminders): Settings → Apps → [app name]
  • Location: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → [app name]
  • Health: Settings → Apps → Health → Data Access & Devices → [app name]

No current data is shown.

Check your internet connection and the app’s permissions. Pull the view down or reopen the app to refresh it. Your Apple Watch transfers sleep data to your iPhone in the morning, which can take a moment.

How do I change an app’s language?

My apps are available in German and English and use your iPhone’s language. To switch, add the second language under Settings → General → Language & Region. After that, you’ll find “Language” under Settings → Apps → [app name].

Where can I find guides for individual features?

In the respective app under Settings. The data sources used are listed there, too.

Are the apps medical products?

No. Health data shown and values calculated from it are not medical statements or diagnoses and are not a substitute for medical advice.
